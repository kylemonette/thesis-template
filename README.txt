This class produces theses and dissertations that follow the formatting guidelines of the Graduate School at
the University of Rhode Island. It generates the title page, approval page, table of contents, and lists of
figures and tables, sets the margins, spacing and page numbering, and produces the List of References and the
Bibliography from a BibTeX database. A sample thesis is included; compile rhode-island-thesis.tex to get started. See
rhode-island-thesis-doc.pdf for the documentation.

This is a modified version of urithesis.cls version 2.0 (2016) by Timothy Toolan, copyright University of
Rhode Island. Timothy Toolan does not support this modified version; report problems to Kyle Monette. The
changes are listed in rhode-island-thesis-doc.pdf. The unmodified version 2.0 is available from
https://www.ele.uri.edu/info/thesis/dist/thesis.zip and is archived at
https://web.archive.org/web/20260216180715/https://www.ele.uri.edu/info/thesis/dist/thesis.zip.
The BibTeX styles uriref.bst, uribib.bst and uriapa.bst keep the licenses stated in each file.
